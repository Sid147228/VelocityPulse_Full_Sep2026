Baseline Method

Run-to-run consistency is checked using Avg, P90 and P95 variation.

Stable means the maximum coefficient of variation across Avg, P90 and P95 is at or below 10%. Variable is 10–20%; Unstable is above 20%. For promotion into a baseline, VelocityPulse uses a stricter rule: at least 3 runs, at least 60% report coverage, P90 data present, and variation at or below 15%. Proposed P90 thresholds use the observed mean plus measured run-to-run variation rather than fixed multipliers.
